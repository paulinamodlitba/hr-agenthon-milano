What this means for the next three hours

  • This is not a beginners' session

    97% of you are past the curious-observer stage, so we skip what a large language model is and start where you actually are: the shift from asking AI for text to building something that does the work.

  • You build in Claude or Lovable, you work in Gemini

    91 of you named Gemini, 27 named Claude, 3 named Lovable. Today is partly about seeing what other tools can do, so you also leave with the bridge back to what you open on Monday morning.

  • Your best use cases need integrations you cannot have today

    Workday configuration, ticket automation, Slack triggers, candidate FAQ bots on a live knowledge base. Nothing connects to production and everything runs on dummy data, so aim for a working prototype that proves the idea rather than a finished system.

  • Time is what you said stops you, so today it is protected

    22 of you named time and trial-and-error as what stops you. Test data and a scoping template are ready, so the first half hour goes into choosing well rather than into setup.

  • You want to see what other teams build

    Learning across teams was your third strongest wish. There are around 26 teams, too many for 26 demos, so the last stretch is built for looking at each other's work rather than sitting through every one of them.
